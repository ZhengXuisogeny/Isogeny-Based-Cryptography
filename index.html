Isogeny-Based Post-Quantum Cryptography

Zheng Xu (徐铮)

Associate Research Professor, Hefei National Laboratory

My research is in computational number theory and post-quantum cryptography, with a focus on computational problems involving isogeny graphs and endomorphism rings of supersingular elliptic curves and abelian varieties. I study how these structures can be used in digital signatures and key exchange, particularly in the SQIsign family of signatures and the module-based isogeny key exchange MIKE.
